01 / ABOUT EMINENCE

Built around crop protection, service and long-term trust.

Eminence Chemical Industries Ltd. says it imports herbicides, insecticides, fungicides and yield boosters from international markets and distributes them throughout Bangladesh.

The company also describes a strong presence in Bangladesh's tea sector and provides products and information to farmers throughout the cultivation process.
